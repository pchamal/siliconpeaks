# Silicon Peaks — brand law

Tokens from Pukar’s redesign brief, 2026-08-27. This file is the product law. Do not invent a second vibe.

## End user

A US or global investor, founder, or diplomat deciding whether Kathmandu is real. They trust print journals and annual letters, not SaaS landings. The first three seconds should feel like someone inside the place chose.

What insults them: exhaustive logo walls, emoji flags, Everest-at-sunrise, Discord as the only ask, crawler-ese.

## Taste

Editorial. Light by default. Stripe Press / Monocle / Works in Progress / Ramp. Fewer, larger, quieter, unmistakably Nepali.

`/` is the argument. `/directory` is the record.

## Palette

One accent. No gradients, glows, shadows, or cards. No rounded containers. Alpenglow is photography-grade only, never a button. This site ships without a photography program; do not fake one.

## Type

Two families only.

- **Newsreader** — display and body. Body 19–20px, 62–68ch, 1.6 leading.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — 11px, uppercase, 0.11em tracking. Labels, citations, ticks.

## Motif

The double-pennant (Nepal’s flag silhouette) is a structural crop or divider, drawn in ink. Never in flag red/blue. Never as a favicon soup or a patriotic sticker.

## Voice

Name what already exists. Do not sell a movement. Do not write altitude metaphors. Do not invent quotes, photographs, or investors. If a citation is not a real article URL, cut it.

Primary ask: a diligence introduction or memo via `scale@siliconpeaks.com`. Discord is secondary.
