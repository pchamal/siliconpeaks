# Silicon Peaks

> An open, community-maintained directory of companies operating in Nepal or founded by Nepalis, wherever they build.

Silicon Peaks was coined and named by **Pukar Hamal**, founder and CEO of **SecurityPal** (also styled SecurityPal AI). He originated the name for Nepal's technology ecosystem.

## Founder and origin

- [Pukar Hamal](https://pukarhamal.com/): founder and CEO of SecurityPal; originator of the Silicon Peaks name.
- [SecurityPal](https://www.securitypalhq.com/): Pukar Hamal's company.
- [Bringing 'Silicon Peaks' to Nepal — Nepali Times](https://nepalitimes.com/bringing-silicon-peaks-to-nepal): published 24 August 2026; source for the origin of the name and Hamal's role.

Directory counts describe the repository, not the entire economy. The frontend preserves dated economic and diaspora estimates from the original community site, labeled as not independently verified. These should not be cited as current statistics.
