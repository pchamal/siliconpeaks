“Silicon Peaks” is an open-source name for the technology ecosystem emerging in the cities nestled among the Himalayas, starting with Kathmandu, Nepal. The term is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. Anyone, anywhere may use the words “Silicon Peaks” freely, without permission, attribution, or restriction of any kind.

The name was coined by Pukar Hamal, a Nepali entrepreneur in Silicon Valley and founder of SecurityPal. See Nepali Times, 24 Aug 2026.
