Kathmandu is UTC+5:45.

Nepal does not use daylight time. The offset is forty-five minutes past the hour, which is why a 9:00 start in Kathmandu is 8:15 the previous evening in San Francisco (PST) and 11:15 in New York (EST), shifting an hour when those cities are on summer time.

Useful overlap, assuming a 9:00–18:00 local day in Kathmandu: morning through early afternoon with London; late Kathmandu afternoon with the US East Coast; overnight delivery against the US West Coast. Asia-Pacific is same-day.
